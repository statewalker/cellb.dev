Astro, built in the browser, on CellB

Each app is compiled in this tab (esbuild and Astro's compiler as WebAssembly, packages from the npm registry), runs on CellB and is served from the tab at this origin's root. Its build is saved in this browser: launching it again runs the saved build without compiling, until it is rebuilt. One app runs at a time: launching the other one replaces it, and each app keeps its own data.
